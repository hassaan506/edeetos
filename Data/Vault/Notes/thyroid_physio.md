# Physiology of the Thyroid Gland

Thyroid hormones regulate the basal metabolic rate and are critical for neurological development.

## Hypothalamic Pituitary Thyroid Axis
* Hypothalamus releases TRH.
* Anterior pituitary releases TSH.
* Thyroid gland releases T3 and T4.
* High levels of T3 and T4 create a negative feedback loop to inhibit TRH and TSH.
