# Forensic Medicine: Thyroid Crisis

Investigating sudden death secondary to endocrine emergencies.

## Thyrotoxic Crisis
A rare but fatal complication of uncontrolled hyperthyroidism.

* **Autopsy Findings:** May be entirely nonspecific. 
* **Diagnosis:** Relies heavily on antemortem clinical history, elevated postmortem T3/T4 levels in vitreous humor, and exclusion of other causes of sudden cardiac death.
