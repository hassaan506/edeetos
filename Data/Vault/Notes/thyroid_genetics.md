# Genetics of Thyroid Neoplasms

Familial syndromes associated with thyroid cancer.

## Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia
Medullary thyroid carcinoma is highly associated with MEN 2A and MEN 2B syndromes.

* **Gene Mutation:** Activating mutation in the RET proto-oncogene.
* **Screening:** Prophylactic thyroidectomy is recommended for family members carrying the RET mutation.
