# Anatomy of the Thyroid Gland

The thyroid gland is a highly vascular endocrine organ located in the neck, anterior to the trachea.

## Key Relations
* **Anterior:** Infrahyoid muscles and deep cervical fascia.
* **Posterior:** Parathyroid glands, recurrent laryngeal nerve, and trachea.

## Blood Supply
* **Arterial:** Superior and inferior thyroid arteries.
* **Venous:** Superior, middle, and inferior thyroid veins.
