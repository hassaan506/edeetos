# Surgery of the Thyroid Gland

Surgical indications and operative complications.

## Indications for Thyroidectomy
* Suspected or confirmed malignancy.
* Compressive symptoms causing dysphagia or stridor.
* Toxic multinodular goiter failing medical therapy.

## Operative Complications
* Recurrent laryngeal nerve injury causing hoarseness.
* Accidental removal of parathyroid glands leading to hypocalcemia.
