# Pathology of the Thyroid Gland

Morphological changes seen in common thyroid disorders.

## Hashimoto Thyroiditis
* Autoimmune destruction of the gland.
* **Histology:** Massive lymphocytic infiltrate with germinal centers and Hurthle cells.

## Papillary Thyroid Carcinoma
* Most common primary thyroid malignancy.
* **Histology:** Orphan Annie eye nuclei, psammoma bodies, and nuclear grooves.
