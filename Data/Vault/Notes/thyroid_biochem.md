# Biochemistry of Thyroid Hormones

Understanding the synthesis and molecular structure of T3 and T4.

## Synthesis Steps
1. Iodide trapping via the Sodium/Iodide symporter.
2. Oxidation of iodide to iodine by thyroid peroxidase.
3. Organification of iodine into tyrosine residues on thyroglobulin.
4. Coupling of MIT and DIT to form T3 and T4.

**Important Enzyme:** Thyroid peroxidase is the primary target for thioamide drugs.
