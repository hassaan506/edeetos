Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Welcome to EDEETOS

We respect your privacy. This page simply explains what information we collect when you use our website, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

2. What We Collect

To make the app work for you, we collect a few things:

  • Your Basic Info: Things like your name, email, university, and phone number so you can log in.
  • Your Study Progress: We track which questions you get right or wrong, your bookmarks, the notes you write, and your exam scores.
  • Payment Receipts: If you buy Premium by sending a bank transfer, you will upload a picture of your receipt. We keep this safely on our servers just to verify your payment.
  • Group Study & Friends: If you study in a group, we temporarily share your answers with your friends in that room. If you challenge a friend, we save your score so they can try to beat it.
  • Chats with Mentors: Live chats with our doctors are completely private. As soon as you close the chat window, the messages are deleted forever.

3. Saving Data to Your Device

EDEETOS is built to be incredibly fast. To do this, we save your study progress and question data directly to your phone or computer. This means the app will load instantly and won't crash if your internet drops.

4. How We Use Your Info

  • To Help You Study: We look at the questions you get wrong so the app can automatically build quizzes to test your weak spots.
  • To Fix Mistakes: If you report a broken question or write a helpful note, we might use that feedback to improve the question bank for everyone.
  • For Mentor Advice: If you talk to a mentor, they can see your past exam scores so they know exactly how to help you.

5. Security & Deleting Your Data

We work hard to keep your information safe. However, if a user tries to hack the site or steal our questions, we will ban their account and our system will automatically delete the offline EDEETOS data saved on their device.

You are always in control of your own progress. If you want a fresh start, you can click "Wipe Data" in your dashboard. This will permanently delete your mistakes, bookmarks, and exam history. Once deleted, you cannot get it back.

6. Questions?

If you have any questions about your privacy, just send us an email at edeetos@gmail.com.
