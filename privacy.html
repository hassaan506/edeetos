Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EDEETOS. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our platform, including our mentorship dashboards and multiplayer study features.

2. Data We Collect

To provide our advanced educational tools, we collect the following types of data:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Name, email address, username, university/institution, location, and WhatsApp numbers (if provided via our contact forms).
  • Granular Educational Data: Your officially assigned exam course, active Premium book add-ons, specific questions solved, mistakes made, bookmarks, exam history, and calculated spaced-repetition intervals.
  • Multiplayer & Sync Data: When using "Group Study" mode, we temporarily store your active session status and answers to synchronize the experience with other users in your room.
  • Device & Cache Data: We utilize browser Local Storage to cache quiz configurations, assigned courses, and data to improve performance.

3. How We Use & Share Your Data

Your data is strictly used to enhance your educational journey. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Smart Performance Analytics: We process your answering history to calculate specific Strengths and Weaknesses. This data powers the Smart Training Hub, allowing us to generate custom algorithmic sessions (such as Redemption Mode, Targeted Focus, and Balanced Mix) to optimize your study time.
  • Mentor Oversight: Authorized EDEETOS Mentors and Admins are granted comprehensive access to your performance data (exam history, specific mistakes, and subject proficiency) to provide targeted guidance and assign customized exams.
  • Manage account security, course assignment requests, subscription tiers, and prevent unauthorized account sharing.

4. Data Security & Progress Reset

We employ robust security measures to prevent your data from being compromised. Additionally, you have the right to request a complete or partial deletion of your educational progress via the "Reset Progress" tool in your dashboard. Please note that data deleted via this tool cannot be recovered.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled by our mentors, please contact us at edeetos@gmail.com.
