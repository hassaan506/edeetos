Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EDEETOS. We respect your privacy and want you to know exactly how we handle your data. This policy explains what information we collect as you study, take exams, and use our mentorship tools, and how we protect it.

2. The Data We Collect

To make our study tools work, we collect the following information:

  • Basic Account Info: Your name, email, username, university, location, and phone number.
  • Your Study Progress: We track your assigned course, the questions you answer, the mistakes you make, your bookmarks, personal notes, and exam scores.
  • Group Study & Challenges: When you use Group Study, we temporarily sync your screen and answers with your friends in the room. When you create a challenge, we save your exam score and timer so your friends can race against it.
  • Private Mentor Chats: Live chats with our mentors are completely private and temporary. The moment you close a chat session, the messages are permanently deleted from our system.
  • Offline Device Saving: We save your current quiz and study progress directly to your phone or computer's local storage. This keeps the website fast and ensures you don't lose your progress if your internet disconnects.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used entirely to help you pass your exams. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Find Your Weaknesses: We analyze your mistakes to power the Smart Training Hub, allowing the system to automatically generate quizzes focused on the topics you struggle with most.
  • Help Mentors Help You: If you work with an EDEETOS mentor, they can securely view your exam history and mistake patterns so they can give you accurate, personalized advice.
  • Manage Your Access: We use your account info to manage your premium subscriptions, unlocked books, and course assignments.

4. Security & Resetting Your Progress

We keep your data secure. However, if an account is banned for breaking our rules (like trying to steal questions), we will automatically delete the offline study data saved on that device to protect our platform.

You have full control over your study data. If you want a fresh start, you can use the "Reset Progress" tool in your dashboard to permanently delete your mistakes, bookmarks, or exam history. Once deleted, this progress cannot be recovered.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about your privacy or how your data is used, please email us at edeetos@gmail.com.
