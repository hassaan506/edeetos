v1.3.0 — Clinical Redesign & 19k Expansion
NOV 2026
  • NEW19,000 Question Bank: We added over 19,000 verified FCPS Part 1 MCQs. Every question is fully categorized by subject, chapter, difficulty, and past exam dates.
  • NEWClinical SaaS Design: We completely rebuilt the interface. All soft, blurry designs are gone. The platform now uses a strict, sharp grid that looks and feels like a professional medical instrument.
  • PERFLightning Fast Loading: We changed how the database stores your study data. Even with 19,000 questions, your dashboard and subjects now load instantly without freezing your phone or browser.
  • PERFInstant Search: Searching for a specific topic or keyword is now instant. It runs entirely in your browser without waiting for a server response.
  • UIHigh-Contrast Colors: We introduced a new Midnight Black (Light Mode) and Vibrant Golden Yellow (Dark Mode) color scheme to make reading and navigation crystal clear.
  • UIBetter Reading Fonts: Medical cases are now displayed in a highly readable 'Inter' font, while test data and timers use a technical 'Courier New' font for precision.
  • UIMechanical Buttons: Buttons now simulate a physical mechanical press when you click them, and dashboard cards smoothly track your mouse movement.
