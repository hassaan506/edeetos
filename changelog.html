Platform Updates

See what's new, what's improved, and what problems we've fixed in EDEETOS.

September 28, 2026

v1.2.0 - Dark Mode & New Design

Major Update
  • New Dark Mode: You can now use EDEETOS in Dark Mode, making studying more comfortable in low-light environments.
  • Design New Look: The website has been given a completely refreshed design with cleaner text, better colours, smoother animations, and a more modern appearance.
  • New Claim Your Rewards Manually: Rewards are no longer shown through a popup that disappears. You now have a permanent "Claim Reward" button in the Journey section, so you can collect your reward whenever you're ready.
  • New Notes Vault: Your saved notes now show the related question and clinical case directly above your notes, making revision easier.
  • Fixed Copying Text on Mobile: Fixed an issue where copying text could sometimes cause the quiz to crash on certain mobile browsers.
  • Fixed Offline Data Loading: Fixed an issue where bookmarks, mistakes, and notes might not load before a quiz started. Your saved study data should now be ready when you begin.
September 27, 2026

v1.1.3 - Ghost Races & Quiz Improvements

Major Update
  • New Ghost Races: After finishing an exam, you can now create a 5-letter challenge code. Friends who join can see your progress and try to complete the exam at the same pace as you.
  • New Quick Mock Exams: You can now create a mock exam directly from the search bar. EDEETOS will automatically select a custom mix of questions you have not attempted yet and add a timer.
  • New Better Performance Charts: Your Performance Report now includes interactive graphs so you can easily see how your exam scores change over time.
  • New Review Your Mistakes: After finishing an exam, you can now press "Review Mistakes" to start a practice session containing only the questions you got wrong.
  • New Notes Vault: All your saved question notes are now collected in one place on the dashboard.
  • New Search and Practice: Search for any topic or keyword and quickly turn the results into a 50-question practice session.
  • New Exam Stress Timer: When less than 5 minutes remain in an exam, the timer now turns red and starts pulsing to create a more realistic exam-pressure experience.
  • Performance Faster Question Loading: EDEETOS can now handle very large question banks much more reliably. This helps prevent mobile browsers from crashing when working with 10,000+ questions.
  • Security Offline Access Protection: If an account is banned, its saved offline study data is automatically removed from the device to prevent continued unauthorized access.
September 27, 2026

v1.1.2 - More Questions & Better Tracking

  • Content Huge FCPS Question Bank Expansion: The FCPS question bank has increased from 7,350 to 13,102 high-yield questions.
  • New Pause During Exams: You can now properly pause an exam. The timer stops and the question is hidden while you take a break.
  • New Your Eliminated Options Are Saved: When you cross out an option and move to another question, your crossed-out choices are remembered when you come back.
  • New Average Time Per Question: Recent Exams now shows how much time you spent on each question on average.
  • Performance Faster Analytics and Chat: Large amounts of study data can now be processed more efficiently, and Mentor Chat has been improved so new messages appear without the screen repeatedly flashing.
  • Fixed Practice and Exam Order: Practice Mode now follows the order of your selected material, helping you study in a structured way. Exam Mode continues to shuffle the questions.
  • Fixed More Accurate Statistics: Fixed several issues that could give incorrect study statistics, including unattempted questions affecting revision, unanswered questions at exam timeout, and corrected answers being counted incorrectly on a second attempt.
  • Fixed Session Safety: Fixed issues with Mentor assignments, saving an exam halfway through, and Ghost study rooms when the host closes the browser.
  • Security Safer Account Records: Banning a user no longer removes their subscription history. Mentor Chat transcripts are also now given enough time to download properly when a chat ends.
September 13, 2026

v1.1.1 - FCPS Question Bank Expansion

  • Content FCPS Question Bank Expanded: The main FCPS question bank has been expanded and verified to contain 7,350 high-yield questions.
  • New Past Exam History: In Practice Mode, you can now see which past exams and years a question appeared in before. These details remain hidden during Exam Mode.
  • Fixed Past Paper Organization: Questions that appeared in multiple years are now placed correctly under all relevant past-paper years without creating duplicate questions in the main Subject or System sections.
July 14, 2026

v1.1.0 - Rewards & Account Improvements

  • Security Reward Protection: Fixed an issue where resetting progress or clearing data could allow the same milestone reward to be claimed more than once.
  • New Instant Book Rewards: When you unlock a book as a milestone reward, it is now added to your account immediately without waiting for an admin.
  • New Smarter Reward Choices: The reward screen now checks what you already own and hides books or courses you already have lifetime access to.
  • New Forgot Password: You can now reset your password directly from the login page using your email.
  • New Reward Reminder: If you close the reward window without selecting a reward, your pending reward will be saved and shown on your dashboard later.
  • Design Simpler Reward Screen: The milestone reward window has been redesigned into one simple menu where you can choose between available rewards more easily.
  • Fixed Correct Milestone Counting: Fixed the way flawless answers are counted so milestone rewards are triggered correctly.
  • Fixed False Session Warnings: Fixed an issue where leaving a quiz normally could incorrectly show an "unexpected session ended" warning the next time you visited.

v1.0.9 - Journey Rewards & Mentor Improvements

  • New Journey Rewards: Your Trophy progress can now unlock Premium Subscription time when you reach certain milestones by answering questions without making mistakes. Rewards can reach up to 3 weeks.
  • New Milestone Notifications: When you reach a flawless-answer milestone, EDEETOS now shows your unlocked reward and achievement.
  • Fixed Mentor Session Setup: Fixed an issue where creating a Mentor Practice Session could incorrectly ask for an exam time limit.
July 12, 2026

v1.0.8 - Better Clinical Quiz Tools

  • New Highlight and Cross Out Text: You can now highlight important parts of a clinical case and cross out distracting information while solving questions.
  • New Better Time Tracking: EDEETOS now keeps track of both your total session time and the time spent on each individual question.
  • New Better Hints: Hints are now specific to the question instead of being generic. A useful clue can appear after 15 seconds.
  • Content Clinical Lab Reference: A complete FCPS/MRCS-level reference for CBC, LFTs, ABGs, electrolytes, and other common laboratory values is now available directly during quizzes.
  • Security Admin Access Improved: Admin users can now copy text and take screenshots when needed for administrative work, while these actions remain restricted for students during quizzes.
  • Design Improved Quiz Screen: The navigation bar, timer, and laboratory reference have been made easier to read. You can also move directly to the next question from the explanation window.
